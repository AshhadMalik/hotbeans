Job Requirements

Qualifications

  • Minimum of 5 GCSEs grade 5 or above.
  • Minimum of 1 A-level above a C.
  • Preferably a University Degree relating to coding, web design, etc.
  • Evidence of experience with HTML, CSS and JavaScript at the minimum.
  • (We also accept qualifications equivalent to the ones listed above)

Requirements

  • Must be 18 or over.
  • Preferably live in the UK however we can work with people outside of the UK.
  • Minimum of 1 month work experience.
  • Ability to work in a team.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Accreditations and certifications will be needed as proof.
  • High level of computer literacy.
  • Strong problem solving skills.
